REQUIREMENTS ANALYSIS OUTPUT
============================================================

1. Functional Requirements
- Patients can register with their name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
- Patients can view available time slots by dentist and location.
- Patients can book appointments for cleaning, checkup, or emergency visits.
- Patients can reschedule and cancel appointments online.
- The system sends email confirmation to patients after booking an appointment.
- The system sends SMS reminders to patients 24 hours before their appointment.
- Clinic staff have an admin dashboard to manage schedules.
- Clinic staff can block off holidays on the calendar.
- Clinic staff can view daily appointment lists.
- The system integrates with the existing patient records system (Dentrix).

2. Non-functional Requirements
- The website must be mobile-friendly to accommodate most patients booking from their phones.
- The system must be available and accessible within 3 months of project initiation.
- The system should have a budget of around $25,000.
- The system must ensure patient data security and comply with relevant healthcare regulations.
- The system should have a user-friendly interface for both patients and clinic staff.
- The system should have a reasonable response time to ensure smooth user experience.

3. Risks
- Technical risk: Integration with Dentrix may be challenging if the API is not available or is complex.
- Business risk: Delays in development may impact the clinic's ability to manage appointments efficiently.
- Timeline risk: The 3-month deadline may be too tight, considering the scope of the project.
- Dependency risk: The project's success relies on the availability and cooperation of the Dentrix API team.

4. Assumptions
- We assume that the Dentrix API is available for integration, but its complexity and documentation are unknown.
- We assume that the clinic staff will receive training on using the admin dashboard and other features of the system.
- We assume that the patient records system (Dentrix) has a standardized data format for integration.
- We assume that the clinic has the necessary infrastructure (e.g., servers, network) to support the new system.

5. Questions to Client
- Can you provide more information about the Dentrix API, such as its availability, documentation, and any existing integrations?
- What is the current process for managing appointments, and how do you envision the new system improving it?
- Are there any specific security or compliance requirements that the system must adhere to?
- Can you provide a detailed breakdown of the budget and any constraints or priorities?
- How will the clinic staff be trained to use the new system, and what support will be needed during the transition?
